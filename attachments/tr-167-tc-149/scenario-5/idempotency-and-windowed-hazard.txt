## Verdict: PASS por diseño + fixture invariants + PR body cross-tenant measurements

### Semántica idempotente (code review + fixture assertions)

`charge_without_order.sql` es 100% derivada de source data (Altru PKs, timestamps del payment, y
un ROW_NUMBER determinístico). Sin state, sin randomness, sin `NEWID()`, sin `SYSDATETIME()`,
sin `RAND()`. Correr el query dos veces sobre el mismo dataset unwindowed produce output byte-a-byte
idéntico porque:

- `ft.ID`, `chargeRevenue.ID`, `SALESORDER.ID`, `SALESORDERPAYMENT.PAYMENTID`, `rgi.ID`,
  `rmft.MembershipTransactionID`, `mri.RecurringInstallmentID` son PKs de Altru — inmutables entre corridas.
- `APPLICATION`, `TYPE`, `DESIGNATIONID`, `TYPECODE` de `REVENUESPLIT_EXT` y `FINANCIALTRANSACTIONLINEITEM`
  son propiedades del row source — inmutables entre corridas.
- Las CTEs nuevas (`RentalPayments`, `PledgeTransactions`, `MembershipPos`, `DonationTransactions`,
  `DonationEligibleLines`, `RecurringInstallmentsByPayment`, `MembershipRecurringInstallments`,
  `SalesOrdersByPayment`, `ResolvedMembershipFinancialTransactions`, `PaymentMethods`) son derivaciones
  puras — todas las branches del COALESCE devuelven el mismo valor si los inputs no cambian.
- `PaymentMethods` reduce `REVENUEPAYMENTMETHOD` a un row por revenue vía `MIN(ID)` guard (F-6) —
  determinístico incluso con múltiples payment methods por revenue.
- `MembershipRecurringInstallments` ranking usa `ORDER BY rmft.MembershipTransactionID, mri.RecurringInstallmentID`
  (F-9) — total ordering, no tied.
- `ChargeRank` ranking hace `ORDER BY` sobre `<total-order preferring rows that resolve a purchase>` —
  determinístico gracias al `PaymentMethods` MIN guard + al total ordering en las CTEs.

### Fixture-backed guardrails

- **Test "reduces REVENUEPAYMENTMETHOD to one row per revenue so the rank is deterministic" (F-6)**:
  pinnea el `MIN(ID)` guard sobre `REVENUEPAYMENTMETHOD`. Sin este guard, el rank sería tied y la
  export sería non-idempotente para cualquier Charge con más de un payment method.

- **Test "breaks the membership installment fan-out with a total ordering" (F-9)**: pinnea que
  `MembershipRecurringInstallments` ordering incluye `mri.RecurringInstallmentID` como tie-breaker.
  Sin esto, membership installments compartirían key ORDER BY constante y el purchase escogido
  fluctuaría entre corridas.

- **Test "emits one Charge per payment and prefers the row that resolves a purchase"**: pinnea que
  `ChargeRank = 1` filter aplica sobre un total-order ranking.

## Windowed-run HAZARD (F-12, DOCUMENTED, NOT FIXED)

**IMPORTANTE**: la garantía "0 dangling references" SOLO aplica sobre corridas UNWINDOWED
(sin `fromDate`/`toDate`).

Del PR body §"Known hazard: windowed runs":

    charge_without_order.sql windows on the PAYMENT date, while each of the five POS Purchase
    queries it references windows on a DIFFERENT date at a DIFFERENT grain:
      - family 1 (rentals): payment date
      - family 2 (sales_order.sql): COALESCE(so.TRANSACTIONDATE, so.DATEADDED)
      - family 3 (pledge): PLEDGE'S OWN COALESCE(ft.CALCULATEDDATE, ft.DATEADDED)
      - family 4 (membership2): COALESCE(installment date, anchor date, mt.TRANSACTIONDATE)
      - family 5 (donation2): ta.CloseDate, the max eligible-line date

    Family 3 (pledge) is the structural break — gift date and payment date are separated BY
    DESIGN, and this file's population is order-less back-office revenue where that separation
    is normal. A pledge raised in 2015 and paid in 2024, run with fromDate = 2024-01-01,
    yields a Charge whose reference names a pledge sales_order_only_pledge.sql filtered out —
    a dangling __r: value, i.e. whole-row rejection, THE EXACT FAILURE IM-1192 REMOVED.

    Reachable from the app (charge-without-order.extractor.ts's mergeFilters() honours
    fromDate/toDate), so a windowed Charge run must be paired with UNWINDOWED POS Purchase runs.
    Aligning the five date grains would change what each POS query emits and needs its own ticket.

## Handoff CRÍTICO al IE (must respect for the LICM re-run)

1. **NO usar `fromDate`/`toDate` en `charge-without-order.extractor.ts`** para la re-corrida de LICM
   después del merge del PR.
2. Si el IE necesita correr un windowed Charge run por cualquier razón, DEBE parear ese run con
   corridas UNWINDOWED de las 5 POS Purchase queries:
   - `pos_purchase_only_rental_payments.sql`
   - `sales_order.sql`
   - `sales_order_only_pledge.sql`
   - `sales_order_only_membership2.sql`
   - `sales_order_only_donation2.sql`
3. Si no se puede garantizar unwindowed en los 5 POS queries, NO correr windowed Charge — resultará
   en whole-row rejections en el load.
4. Aligning the 5 date grains sería otro ticket aparte (fuera del scope de IM-1192).
