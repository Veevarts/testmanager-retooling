Notes:
  · A04 FAIL severity is Medium (not High) because:
      · The current data volume in prd is small (the migration just landed);
        the latency/cost impact is not yet felt.
      · The fix path is well-scoped: swap `.scan(...)` for `.query(...)` on the
        existing GSIs.
      · No exploitable security consequence today; the DoS amplification is a
        theoretical risk that grows with data.
  · Fixing A04 also removes the runtime need for `dynamodb:Scan` IAM
    permission, tightening AC8's least-privilege posture (compound win).
