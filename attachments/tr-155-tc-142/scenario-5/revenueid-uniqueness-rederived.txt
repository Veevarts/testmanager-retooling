Método: safesql, agregados de solo lectura. Sin filas, sin PII, sin wildcard, sin --allow-writes.

===============================================================================
QUERY EJECUTADA (idéntica en los tres perfiles)
===============================================================================
  SELECT
      COUNT(so.REVENUEID)          AS NonNullRevenueIds,
      COUNT(DISTINCT so.REVENUEID) AS DistinctRevenueIds,
      COUNT(so.REVENUEID) - COUNT(DISTINCT so.REVENUEID) AS DuplicateExcess,
      COUNT_BIG(*)                 AS TotalSalesOrders
  FROM SALESORDER so;
