IM-1160 / TC-140 scenario 7 — measuring the documented follow-up
"fund_assignment_memberships.sql still resolves only real sales orders"

This is NOT a defect of PR #163. The ticket lists it under "Follow-up to decide" and the PR
lists it under "Known gap (out of scope, logged on IM-1160)". The test case exists to put a
NUMBER on the disagreement so product can decide with data instead of intuition.

Measured on tucson, 2026-08-03, safesql aggregate-only:

  fund_assignment_memberships.sql
    fa_rows      102524   total membership fund-assignment rows
    fa_pos_null   80585   rows whose vnfp__Opportunity_POS_Purchase__c is NULL
    fa_pos_real   21939   rows resolving a real SALESORDER

  membership_transactions.sql (POST)
    total_opportunities  98950
    pos_synthetic        79327   Opportunities that now carry a synthetic POS Purchase

THE DISAGREEMENT
  80585 membership fund-assignment rows on tucson will export
  vnfp__Opportunity_POS_Purchase__c = NULL while the Opportunity they belong to now
  carries a synthetic POS Purchase. Before this PR both sides were NULL together, so
  they agreed. After this PR they disagree on that population.

  Fund-assignment rows (102524) outnumber Opportunities (98950) because fund assignments
  are at split grain, so the two counters are not expected to match exactly - what matters
  is that a large, non-marginal population is now inconsistent between the two objects.

WHY IT IS NOT A DANGLING REFERENCE
  The fund assignment does not point at a POS Purchase that fails to exist; it simply
  points at nothing. Nothing breaks on load. The risk is semantic: any report, formula or
  UI that joins Opportunity POS Purchase to fund-assignment POS Purchase, or that assumes
  the two agree, will now see a mismatch on ~80.6k rows in this tenant alone.

WHAT QA COULD NOT DECIDE
  Whether the fund assignment SHOULD mirror the fallback is a product call, exactly as the
  ticket states. QA's contribution is the magnitude. Left open deliberately.
