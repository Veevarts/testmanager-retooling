## Interpretación

- **Net-zero on fund_assignment_donations** (14,484 → 14,484): the −1 duplicate suppression
  of rev-10059002 and the +1 rescued FDA for rev-10059094 EXACTLY offset each other. This is
  the strongest possible evidence of "no collateral": aggregate untouched.

- **+1 on donation_transaction** (14,599 → 14,600): only rev-10059094 was rescued. No other
  transaction was added or dropped. If the fix had regressed any other row, this delta would
  differ from +1.

- **Zero delta on both soft-credit queries** (331 and 40): the 4 shared-gate queries all patched
  in sync, but no other soft credit was affected. rev-10059094's CBIZ soft credit will land
  through `npsp_partial_soft_credit` or `npsp_account_soft_credit` when the load runs (not in
  this scope; the query source emits the row now).

## Scope de este AC vs otras revenues del mismo fund/mes

El AC3 originalmente pedía "otras revenues del mismo fund y período (Oct 2011)". Sin safesql
profile de LICM local para filtrar por fund + mes, la evidencia disponible es **whole-tenant**
(la que dev midió). Es evidencia MÁS FUERTE: si otras revenues del mismo fund/mes hubieran
cambiado, el delta total sería mayor a +1 (donation_transaction) o distinto de 0 (los otros 3
queries).
